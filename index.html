What was built

  • Machine learning models that score churn risk from behaviour, usage, and engagement data, shown as a live customer health score.
  • AI Playbooks and a Win Back Engine that start retention campaigns automatically based on risk level and customer segment.

05 — Approach

How I take a product from problem to outcome

A practical loop, not a ceremony. AI is added where it beats a simpler solution, and then measured.

Discover

Talk to customers and stakeholders, look at the data, and find the problem that is worth solving before thinking about features.

Define

Write the problem, users, and success measures into PRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria that engineering and design can build from.

Prioritize

Rank work by business value and technical feasibility. Choose AI features by the value they add, not because they are available.

Build

Work closely with engineers and designers in short iterations: prototype early, design the system and interface, and ship tested releases.

Measure

Track KPIs and run A/B tests where possible. For AI features, check quality in production, including accuracy and hallucinations.

Improve

Feed results back into the roadmap. Keep what works, fix or remove what does not, and repeat.
